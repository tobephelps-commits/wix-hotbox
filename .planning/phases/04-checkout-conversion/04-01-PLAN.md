<objective>
Configure all checkout policies (return, shipping, privacy, terms, contact, custom) via the WIX Checkout Settings API and verify they appear correctly on the checkout page.

Purpose: CK-1 (zero checkout policies) was identified as a top-5 conversion killer in Phase 1. Customers see no return policy, shipping policy, or terms at checkout -- a major trust barrier that causes cart abandonment. Adding policies is fully automatable via the WIX REST API.
Output: All 6 checkout policy slots populated with appropriate content, visible at checkout as clickable footer links.
</objective>
