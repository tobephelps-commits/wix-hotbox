# Phase 14: Logo Overlay Engine - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-01-31
**Status:** Ready for research

<vision>
## How This Should Work

When creating a product, you choose which logo to apply and where it goes on the image. It's per-product, not automatic — full control each time. You pick the logo (BigBarn, Board30, etc.) and the position (top-left, center-chest, bottom-right, etc.), and the engine composites it onto the SanMar product images.

The result should look like a screen print mockup — the logo appears as if it's actually printed on the garment, not just a sticker slapped on top of the photo. Think realistic mockup feel, like what you'd see on a custom apparel site showing "your logo here" previews.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **Professional quality** — The overlay must look clean, properly sized, and polished. Transparency handled correctly, logo not covering important product details. This is the #1 priority.
- **Realistic mockup feel** — Logo should look like it's on the fabric, not floating above it. Screen print style, not watermark style.
- **Per-product control** — Choose which logo and which position each time, not locked to collection defaults.

</essential>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Screen print mockup aesthetic — logo composited to look like it's actually on the garment
- Position selection per product — choose where the logo lands on the image (top-left, center, bottom-right, etc.)
- Logo selection per product — pick from available logos (BigBarn, Board30, etc.) or none
- Proper transparency/alpha blending so the logo integrates with the garment texture

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

Logo files are already ready. The key challenge is making the compositing look professional rather than cheap. The user values quality of output over speed of the workflow — better to take an extra step to get the placement right than to rush and look bad.

</notes>
