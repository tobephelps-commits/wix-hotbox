<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Analyze UX issues across navigation, mobile, and checkout</name>
  <files>.planning/phases/01-site-audit-discovery/UX-ISSUES.md</files>
  <action>
    1. Review SITE-STRUCTURE.md and PRODUCT-CATALOG.md findings
    2. Use the WIX MCP to gather additional data needed for UX assessment:
       - Use SearchWixRESTDocumentation to find APIs for checkout/cart configuration, shipping settings, payment methods
       - Use ReadFullDocsArticle + CallWixSiteAPI to query:
         a. Checkout flow configuration (what steps exist, guest checkout available?)
         b. Shipping options and rules
         c. Payment methods enabled
         d. Any discount/coupon settings
         e. Store policies (return, shipping, privacy pages)
    3. Analyze navigation UX:
       - Can a new visitor find products within 2 clicks from homepage?
       - Is the menu hierarchy logical for the product types sold?
       - Are client landing pages discoverable or hidden?
       - Are there dead-end pages with no navigation back?
       - Is search available and prominent?
    4. Analyze product discovery UX:
       - Can customers filter by size, color, product type?
       - Are categories meaningful or confusing?
       - Do product pages have sufficient info to buy (images, sizing, descriptions)?
       - Is cross-selling or related products shown?
    5. Analyze checkout/conversion:
       - How many steps from "Add to Cart" to order completion?
       - Is guest checkout available?
       - What payment methods are offered?
       - Are trust signals present (SSL, return policy, secure payment badges)?
       - Are shipping costs visible before checkout?
    6. Assess mobile experience implications:
       - Based on site structure: are there elements that would be problematic on mobile? (deep menus, wide tables, small tap targets implied by page structure)
       - Note: Full mobile visual audit requires browser, but structural issues can be identified from the data

    Write UX-ISSUES.md with:
    - **Critical Issues** (likely conversion killers — fix first)
    - **Navigation Issues** (feeds Phase 2)
    - **Mobile Concerns** (feeds Phase 3 — structural issues identifiable from data)
    - **Checkout & Conversion Issues** (feeds Phase 4)
    - **Client Experience Issues** (multi-client landing page problems)
    - **Quick Wins** (easy fixes with high impact)

    Each issue should have: description, severity (critical/high/medium/low), which phase addresses it, and specific evidence from the audit data.
  </action>
  <verify>
    - UX-ISSUES.md exists with all six sections
    - Every issue has severity, target phase, and evidence
    - Checkout configuration data was successfully queried
    - Issues are specific (not "navigation is confusing" but "Main menu has 12 top-level items with no grouping, 3 of which lead to empty categories")
    - Client experience issues are specifically called out per CONTEXT.md requirement
  </verify>
  <done>Prioritized UX issues report with specific, evidence-based findings tied to the audit data, ready to drive Phases 2-4.</done>
</task>
