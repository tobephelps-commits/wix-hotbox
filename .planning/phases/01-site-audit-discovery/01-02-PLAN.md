<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Query and analyze all products and categories</name>
  <files>.planning/phases/01-site-audit-discovery/PRODUCT-CATALOG.md</files>
  <action>
    1. Get site ID from 01-01-SUMMARY.md or SITE-STRUCTURE.md
    2. Use SearchWixRESTDocumentation to find APIs for:
       - Querying/listing all products (Stores Catalog API)
       - Querying product categories/collections
       - Getting product variants and options
    3. Use ReadFullDocsArticle to get exact API schemas
    4. Use CallWixSiteAPI to:
       - List ALL products (paginate if needed — expect 25-100 products per PROJECT.md)
       - List ALL product categories/collections
       - For each product: get full details including variants, options, images, descriptions, pricing, stock status, visibility
    5. For each product, assess:
       - Does it have images? How many? Are they mockup images or blanks?
       - Does it have a description? How detailed?
       - What variants exist (colors, sizes)? Are they consistent?
       - Is pricing set? What range?
       - Is it visible/published or hidden/draft?
       - Which categories/collections is it in?
    6. For categories: map the hierarchy, count products per category, identify empty or near-empty categories
    7. Cross-reference with client landing pages from SITE-STRUCTURE.md — which products appear on which client pages?

    Write PRODUCT-CATALOG.md with these sections:
    - **Catalog Overview** (total products, categories, price range, variant patterns)
    - **Category Structure** (hierarchy, product counts, empty categories)
    - **Product Inventory** (table: name, category, variants count, images count, has description, price range, visibility)
    - **Product Quality Assessment** (products with missing images, empty descriptions, no variants, pricing issues)
    - **Client-Product Mapping** (which products are curated for which client landing pages)
    - **Catalog Issues** (brutally honest: orphan products, inconsistent data, missing info, organizational problems)
  </action>
  <verify>
    - PRODUCT-CATALOG.md exists with all six sections
    - Every product on the site is listed in the inventory
    - Categories are fully mapped with product counts
    - Quality issues are specifically identified per product (not vague generalizations)
    - Client-product mapping cross-references SITE-STRUCTURE.md findings
  </verify>
  <done>Complete product catalog audit with every product inventoried, category structure mapped, quality issues identified per product, and client-product relationships documented.</done>
</task>
